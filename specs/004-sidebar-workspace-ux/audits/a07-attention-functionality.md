## Proposed interaction design

Recommended first slice: **ATT-001 only**. Selecting a record establishes the detail context. Dismissing another row updates that row without moving the detail. Dismissing the selected row chooses a documented adjacent remaining record, or the empty state. Failed dismissal keeps selection and record, with a reachable correction/retry path. Preserve exact IDs and main-owned resolution semantics; replacement stays a separate reviewed launch.

ATT-002 needs an explicit scope choice before extending queue capability. The audit does not claim mission decisions are lost or that its recovery-specific empty message is factually false. ATT-003 and ATT-004 should be reconciled with shared shell/accessibility findings rather than fixed through competing local navigation frameworks. Keep priority/chronology policy explicit if expanding the queue; current runtime pass does not establish severity ordering or large-inventory performance.

## Section acceptance summary

- Visual/interaction: observed selection reset, recovery-only scope discoverability gap, narrow/text-scale overflow and incomplete current-selection/landmark semantics. Standard captures inspected; no full accessibility pass claimed.
- Logic: unresolved-record filtering, exact record targeting and main resolution safeguards traced. Selection fallback causes a reproducible interaction defect; mission decision ownership is distinct from recovery resolution.
- Functionality: dismissal saved/restarted correctly; rejected dismissal leaves authoritative state unchanged. Existing tests verify crash reconciliation, distinct reviewed replacement, old-session stopping and no automatic replay. Local fixture processes are not real provider capability evidence.
- Missing: initial-load failure, all five classifications, no-session record, provider/folder repair, double activation/in-flight selection race, cancel/expired replacement review, real degraded database startup, full keyboard/screen-reader and large-inventory matrix. These remain pending; do not infer completion from passing sampled tests.
- A08 Settings is the next unaudited destination; A09 and prior A01-A07 coverage gaps remain open. Proposed improvements require owner acceptance before implementation tasks.
