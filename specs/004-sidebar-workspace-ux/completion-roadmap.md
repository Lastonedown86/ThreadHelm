## Evidence and PR boundary

For each slice, record the exact main/branch baseline, Windows environment, fixtures and observed UI actions. Keep separate verdicts for UI/interaction, underlying logic and actual functionality. Verify effects independently using saved-state readback, lifecycle/process identity or other authoritative observations appropriate to the action. A toast, successful screenshot capture, source inspection or passing generic suite does not establish functional success.

Each implementation slice needs before/after primary-journey evidence, an applicable failure/recovery path, and cross-section navigation impact (SC-008). Cover stale state, cancellation, repeated activation and restart when applicable; explain exclusions. Keep fixture/injected failures distinct from real boundary observations. Preserve durable evidence or a precise recapture procedure with its limitations.

Once the bounded work and required checks are complete, automatically open a reviewable PR under the owner's standing instruction. Report local verification and hosted CI separately. The owner merges; continue on the previous slice branch while its PR is open, and refresh main and reconcile dependencies when merges arrive. Do not create a recurring automation. A blocked implementation or acceptance gate stays visibly blocked, not complete.

## Completion and scope control

The endpoint is slice 33. Feature completion requires all 18 finding IDs to have verified resolutions or explicit owner-approved dispositions, all required coverage and success criteria reconciled, accepted fixes merged, and the owner shown the remaining limitations. Unexercised required cases remain pending/blocked and prevent an unqualified completion claim. Release, provider autonomy and other feature gates remain separate.

Fresh-main revalidation can close an already-resolved finding with evidence and reduce implementation PRs. If a slice must split, or new defects require additional work, revise this roadmap with the reason, owning finding/requirement, count impact and proposed decision before extending the sequence. Do not silently append slice 34 or defer unmet requirements merely to preserve the count. Serious regressions block acceptance and are fixed in their owning slice or through an explicit roadmap revision.

Durable recon proposal recovery and unified Mission/Attention aggregation remain separate capability decisions. Real external-provider runs retain their existing owner-authorization boundaries; this roadmap grants no additional provider execution or release authority.
